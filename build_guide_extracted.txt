1. About this document
This guide is the single source of content, design direction and build requirements for the AcresxManna website. It is written for the developer, designer and content editor who will build and launch the site.
Core principle: playful voice, precise facts. We are fun, bubbly and energetic in how we communicate, and strictly honest in what we claim. Every page should feel like a celebration of African food while staying credible to buyers, distributors and investors.
How to use this guide
The Business facts section is the source of truth. Where anything else in this guide conflicts with it, the Business facts section wins.
Copy shown in the page sections is production copy unless it carries a tag.
Build with placeholders for anything tagged, and do not publish a placeholder.
Tags used in this guide
[TBC] To be confirmed: we will supply or verify this detail before the content goes live.
[PLANNED] Planned or in development: label it that way on the site, never as existing.
[PHOTO] Needs a real photograph.

2. Truth rules (non-negotiable)
We are a young company. Say we’re building, not we’ve built.
Never publish invented numbers, farmer counts, certifications, testimonials, awards or markets. Every figure needs a source.
Mark existing facilities and products separately from planned ones ([PLANNED]).
Show peanut butter as a product only once it is commercially available.
Show a country as an established market only where sales or distribution are verified. Everything else is a target market.
Say exactly what the UK-facing business and the Nigerian operations each do: which entity manufactures, contracts with customers, exports and invoices buyers. [TBC]
For each brand, say whether it is a separate company, an affiliate or a developing initiative. [TBC]
Do not publish fundraising amounts, valuations, equity terms or financial projections.
Use real photographs of the Nigerian operation. [PHOTO]
3. Brand and voice
Positioning: AcresxManna is an African agro-processing and food manufacturing company focused on transforming agricultural commodities into quality food products for African and international markets.
Primary headline: Africa’s Agricultural Wealth. Transformed for the World.
Tagline: Redefining the African Agro-food Market Globally.
Vision: To become Africa’s leading agro-processing and food manufacturing company, recognised globally for scale, quality, innovation and the value we create from African agriculture.
Mission: To transform African agricultural resources into competitive food products through processing, manufacturing, quality control, brand development and access to markets.
Promise: More value from Africa’s harvest. More opportunities across the food value chain. More African food on the world’s tables.
Voice rules
Warm, upbeat, short sentences. Exclamation marks are welcome, but use one at most per section.
Energy comes from rhythm and verbs (Source. Process. Pack. Ship.), not from big claims.
Do: We’re building the capacity behind African food. Don’t: We are Africa’s number one food company.
Do: Garri, ready for more tables! Don’t: Sold in 20 countries.
Microcopy
Buttons: Let’s Talk · Explore Our Products · Become a Supplier · Become a Distributor · Say Hello
Form success: Yay, message received! The AcresxManna team will get back to you soon. [TBC] Replace soon with a real response time once agreed.
Form error: Oops, that didn’t send. Please try again or email us directly.
Page not found: Oops! This page took a detour. Let’s get you back on track.
Newsletter: Get progress updates, new products and partnership news.
4. Design direction
Palette. Use the official brand colours exactly as supplied: purple, white and yellow.
Brand Purple #594B9A for headers, hero and key sections
White #FFFFFF for page backgrounds and cards
Brand Yellow #FDDC42 for buttons, highlights and accents
Optional derived tints for depth only, such as a darker purple #3F346E for text and shadows and a light lilac #EFEBF8 for card backgrounds [TBC] Needs our approval
Pairing rules: white text on purple passes contrast (about 7 to 1); purple or near-black text on yellow passes; never put yellow text on white (about 1.4 to 1, which fails)
Type. The official brand font is Helvetica Neue: bold for headings, regular for body. It is a licensed font, so confirm a web licence, and set the fallback stack to Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif. Keep the fun in the colours, rounded shapes, motion and copy rather than in the typeface. [TBC]
Shape language. Round everything: pill buttons, 30 to 44px card corners, circle number badges, a wavy divider under the hero and between sections.
Motion (all of it off when the visitor prefers reduced motion)
Slowly floating bubbles in the hero
A scrolling ticker of products
Sticker-style buttons that lift and tilt slightly on hover
Cards that rise on hover; a gentle fade-up as sections enter
Components to build once and reuse
Hero with bubbles · Product ticker · Section heading with eyebrow label
Feature card (icon, title, text) · Product card with status tag
Step row (numbered circles) · Brand card · Role tile (who are you?)
CTA band · Form with enquiry-type selector · Status tags: Available, In development, Target market
Imagery. Real photos of production, equipment, packaging and finished products come first [PHOTO]. Where no photo exists yet, use friendly flat illustration or the mascot. Never use stock photos that imply facilities or markets that don’t exist.
Accessibility and performance
Text contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 (purple on gold and white on purple both pass)
Visible focus rings, a skip link, alt text that describes the image (not file names)
Mobile first; no sideways scrolling; tap targets of at least 44px
Compress images, lazy-load below the fold, and keep each page light
Set noindex on any staging site until launch
5. Sitemap and navigation
Tabs, in order: Home · About Us · What We Do · Products · Our Brands · Infrastructure · Impact · Global Markets · Investors & Partners · News & Insights · Contact
Header button: Let’s Talk (goes to Contact)
Eleven tabs is a lot for a header. On desktop show the first six plus a More menu. On mobile use a full-screen menu with big, friendly tap targets.
Page purposes: Home introduces the vision; About explains the company; What We Do shows capabilities; Products shows the portfolio; Our Brands shows related ventures; Infrastructure shows operating capacity; Impact covers value creation; Global Markets covers international ambitions; Investors & Partners supports commercial talks; News hosts updates; Contact captures enquiries.

22.2 Style rules
Spelling: British English (organised, flavour, programme). Use it everywhere for consistency across the Nigerian and UK audiences.
Names: AcresxManna (one word, capital A and M, lower-case x). Brands: HappyMan Foods, Maluza Foods Co., UncleTobi.
Headlines: sentence case, except the Home hero which is upper case by design. Keep them under 12 words.
Buttons: start with a verb, two to four words, for example Explore Our Products.
Numbers: write one to nine in words, 10 and above as digits; always include the unit.
Dates: 9 October 2026 style, with no ordinals.
Punctuation: at most one exclamation mark per section; avoid ellipses and all caps outside the hero.
Emojis: only in the places this guide shows them (thank-you, 404, cookie banner and a few playful accents); never in legal pages or formal documents.
Tone check: read each section aloud. It should sound like a friendly, confident person who knows the business well and doesn’t oversell.
22.3 Words to avoid
Leading, largest, number one and similar superlatives, unless the claim is verified and sourced
Luxury, premium and fine dining, which clash with the everyday and accessible brand idea
Certified, approved and guaranteed, unless true and documented
Exporting to or available in followed by a country, unless verified
Thousands of farmers, tonnes per year and any other number without a source
22.4 Final reminder
If a sentence could not be defended in front of a buyer, a regulator or an investor, remove it. Playful voice, precise facts.
